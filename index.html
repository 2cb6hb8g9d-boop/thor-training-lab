Progression rules

Per exercise adjustable
1. Rep progression

Add reps while staying inside the prescribed range and effort target.

2. Load progression

Increase load when all working sets reach the top of the range at or easier than target RIR.

3. Fatigue management

If reps fall or RIR is materially lower, hold load or review fatigue and technique.
